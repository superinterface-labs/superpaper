## 40 questions

> From [Steph Ango's template](https://stephango.com/40-questions). Answer as many as feel right.

1. What did you do this year that you'd never done before?
2. Did you keep your resolutions, and will you make more for next year?
3. Did anyone close to you give birth?
4. Did anyone close to you die?
5. What places did you visit?
6. What would you like to have next year that you lacked this year?
7. What date from this year will remain etched upon your memory?
8. What was your biggest achievement of the year?
9. What was your biggest failure?
10. What other hardships did you face?
11. Did you suffer illness or injury?
12. What was the best thing you bought?
13. Whose behavior merited celebration?
14. Whose behavior made you appalled?
15. Where did most of your money go?
16. What did you get really, really, really excited about?
17. What song will always remind you of this year?
18. Compared to this time last year, are you: happier or sadder? Thinner or fatter? Richer or poorer?
19. What do you wish you'd done more of?
20. What do you wish you'd done less of?
21. How did you spend the holidays?
22. What was your favorite TV program?
23. Do you hate anyone now that you didn't hate this time last year?
24. What was the best book you read?
25. What was your greatest musical discovery of the year?
26. What did you want and get?
27. What did you want and not get?
28. What was your favorite film?
29. What one thing made your year immeasurably more satisfying?
30. How would you describe your personal fashion this year?
31. What kept you sane?
32. Which celebrity/public figure did you fancy the most?
33. What political issue stirred you the most?
34. Who did you miss?
35. Who was the best new person you met?
36. What was the best thing you ate?
37. Tell us a valuable life lesson you learned this year.
38. What is a quote that sums up your year?
39. What are you most looking forward to next year?
40. What are you least looking forward to next year?
